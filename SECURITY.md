# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue.** Report it privately, in either of these ways:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** — the preferred route. Go to the
   [Security tab](https://github.com/niway-dev/kaipu-record/security) and choose
   **Report a vulnerability**.
2. **Email** — [contacto@niway.dev](mailto:contacto@niway.dev), with "Security" in the
   subject.

Include what is affected (the desktop app, the website, or the Cloud API), the version, the
steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has.

## What to expect

Kaipu is maintained by one person ([@csdev19](https://github.com/csdev19)). You will get an
acknowledgement within a few days. A fix ships in the next release once confirmed, and you
are credited in the release notes unless you ask not to be.

## Scope

- **Kaipu Record for macOS** — the latest release. Older releases are not patched; the app
  updates itself.
- **kaipu.app** and the Kaipu Cloud API.

Out of scope: findings that need a compromised machine or physical access, and reports
generated by automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
