FAQ

Before you install.

What can I do locally?

Record, annotate screenshots, edit, and export on your Mac.

Do I need an account?

Not to record, edit, or export locally. An account is used for optional cloud capabilities.

Where are my files?

In your local library folder. Open it in Finder and use the files outside Kaipu.

Does it work on my computer?

Kaipu is available for Mac with separate Apple Silicon and Intel downloads. Follow the roadmap for Windows progress.
