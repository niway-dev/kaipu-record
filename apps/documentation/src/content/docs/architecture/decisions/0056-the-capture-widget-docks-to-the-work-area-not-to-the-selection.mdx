## Context

The capture overlay was four pieces that follow the selection box: an action bar under the box
(screenshot), a vertical panel on the box's right edge and a ratio bar above it (recording), and a
hint line pinned to the screen bottom, far from the controls and drawn over other apps' text.
They spread out, overlapped the content and each other, and the control that matters, the start
button, was a dot with no text. The owner tested that version and rejected it.

## Decision

1. **One toolbar for both intents**, with the same structure: capture-as, area and ratio on the
   first row; the intent's inputs and the confirm button on the second; key hints inside it on the
   third.
2. **It docks to the display's work area**, centred, 16 px above the bottom edge. The work area
   already excludes the Dock, the taskbar and the menu bar.
3. **Jump rule.** If the selection reaches the band the widget would occupy, the widget moves to
   the top of the work area, 16 px below its top edge. It returns when the box leaves the band.
4. **No free edge.** In Screen mode, or with a Frame box that spans the full height, the widget
   stays at the bottom and covers that strip. The widget is hidden before capture (0022), so the
   strip is in the image or video. This is accepted.
5. **The countdown runs inside the widget**, which stays visible and dims; the overlay starts
   the take at zero.
6. **The old pieces are deleted:** the selection toolbar, the record widget, the ratio bar and
   the pinned hint pill.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Direction B, a side panel,** and **direction C, a compact bar,** of the commissioned mock: the
  owner chose A.
- **Restyling the existing pieces in place:** the problem is that there are several pieces that
  follow the box, not how each looks.
- **A translucent or collapsed widget for full-screen selections:** a second state to learn for a
  case the owner accepts as covering a strip.

## Consequences

- The widget's position depends on the display's work area and the box, not on a measured
  selection edge, so the placement is a pure function a test pins.
- In Screen mode every display shows its own widget.
- The record-capture-modes placement (right edge) no longer exists; ratios now show in the
  screenshot intent too.

## Non-goals

Window mode (stays disabled, "soon"), editable W × H and size presets, a compact or collapsed
widget.

## Reopen when

The owner finds that the bottom position covers what they need to see in common captures, or a
compact mode is requested.
